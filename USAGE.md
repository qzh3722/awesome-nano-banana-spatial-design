# Usage Principles

This repository is open-sourced to encourage learning, adaptation, and collaboration in the field of AI-assisted spatial design.

## Before You Use

Please note:

- Prompts here are **working templates**, not guaranteed solutions
- Blind copying is discouraged — every project has unique contexts
- Modification based on your specific project requirements is strongly recommended
- AI output should always be reviewed and validated by a professional designer before being used in real projects or client communication

## What You CAN Do

✅ Use these prompts in personal or commercial projects  
✅ Modify and adapt them for your own workflows  
✅ Share derivatives with proper attribution  
✅ Learn from the methodology and create your own prompt library  

## What We Ask You NOT To Do

❌ Repackage this repository as a paid "prompt product"  
❌ Present the prompts as proprietary or "exclusive formulas"  
❌ Remove attribution when redistributing
