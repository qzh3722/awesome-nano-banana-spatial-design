## Phase 2: Content Sprint - Architecture (Week 2)

**Status**: 📋 Planned

**Objectives**:
- [ ] Complete 30 architecture cases across all subcategories:
  - 8 cases: CAD to Visualization (A-001 to A-008)
  - 8 cases: Concept Development (A-009 to A-016)
  - 8 cases: Material & Facade Studies (A-017 to A-024)
  - 6 cases: Structural & Technical Analysis (A-025 to A-030)

**Process per case**:
1. Write and test English prompt (3-5 generations)
2. Generate high-quality example outputs
3. Write Chinese translation
4. Document parameters and tips
5. Review and refine

**Success Criteria**:
- All prompts tested and produce consistent results
- Example images minimum 1920px, professional quality
- Both language versions complete and accurate
