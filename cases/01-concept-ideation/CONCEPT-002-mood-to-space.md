## Tips / 使用技巧

### 中文

- **色彩精确度**: 上传情绪板作为Style Reference，设置强度0.6-0.8以确保色调一致
- **材质对应**: 明确列出"情绪板中的铜色金属 → 黄铜灯具"这样的对应关系
- **氛围关键词**: 使用"Moody"、"Hygge"、"Wabi-Sabi"等专业术语精准控制氛围
- **分步骤生成**: 先生成整体空间，再用Inpainting添加装饰细节(抱枕、艺术品)

### English

- **Color Accuracy**: Upload mood board as Style Reference with strength 0.6-0.8 for consistent color palette
- **Material Mapping**: Clearly map "copper tones in mood board → brass light fixtures" relationships
- **Atmosphere Keywords**: Use professional terms like "Moody", "Hygge", "Wabi-Sabi" for precise control
- **Phased Generation**: Generate overall space first, then add decorative details (cushions, artwork) via Inpainting
