# Technical to Visual / 技术转可视化

> CAD/drawings to photorealistic visualization | CAD/图纸转照片级可视化

## 工作流阶段说明 / Workflow Stage Description

### 中文

**技术转可视化**是设计流程的第三阶段，将技术图纸（CAD平面图、立面图、剖面图）转换为照片级真实的可视化效果图。这个阶段帮助设计师快速将技术图纸转化为客户易于理解的视觉呈现。

**适用场景**:
- CAD黑白平面图转彩色带家具的俯视图
- 立面图转3D真实渲染效果
- 剖面图转室内空间可视化
- 手绘草图转专业演示级效果图
- 轴测图转真实视角效果图

**典型用例**:
- CAD平面图转照片级俯视图（带材质和家具）
- 建筑立面图转街景效果图
- 景观总平面图转鸟瞰可视化（带真实植物）
- 室内剖面图转空间透视图
- 技术细节图转特写渲染图

### English

**Technical to Visual** is the third stage of the design workflow, converting technical drawings (CAD floor plans, elevations, sections) into photorealistic visualizations. This stage helps designers quickly transform technical drawings into visual presentations that clients can easily understand.

**Use Cases**:
- Convert black-and-white CAD floor plans to colored top-down views with furniture
- Transform elevations into 3D realistic renders
- Convert sections into interior space visualizations
- Turn hand sketches into professional presentation-quality renders
- Transform axonometric drawings into realistic perspective views

**Typical Examples**:
- CAD floor plan to photorealistic top-down view (with materials and furniture)
- Architectural elevation to street view render
- Landscape master plan to bird's-eye visualization (with realistic plants)
- Interior section to spatial perspective view
- Technical detail drawing to close-up render
