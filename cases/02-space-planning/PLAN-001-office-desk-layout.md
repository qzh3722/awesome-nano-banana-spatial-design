## Tips / 使用技巧

### 中文

- **人体工程学验证**: 提示词中明确"确保工位间距1.2米"可避免生成过于拥挤的布局
- **启用Thinking Mode**: 对于复杂的柱网和不规则空间，启用思考模式帮助AI理解空间约束
- **动线优化**: 使用"主通道1.5米宽"等具体数值，而不是模糊的"宽敞通道"
- **多方案对比**: 通过改变"行列式排列"vs"岛式分组"快速生成2-3种布局方案

### English

- **Ergonomic Validation**: Specifying "ensure 1.2m spacing between desks" prevents overcrowded layouts
- **Enable Thinking Mode**: For complex column grids and irregular spaces, enable thinking mode to help AI understand spatial constraints
- **Circulation Optimization**: Use specific values like "1.5m wide main corridors" instead of vague "spacious pathways"
- **Multiple Options**: Generate 2-3 layout variations by changing "row arrangement" vs "clustered islands"
