# Material & Styling / 材质软装

> Material refinement & styling details | 材质细化与软装搭配

## 工作流阶段说明 / Workflow Stage Description

### 中文

**材质软装**是设计流程的第四阶段，专注于材质细化、色彩方案、软装配饰和风格定制。这个阶段帮助设计师在已有空间基础上进行材质和风格的深化设计。

**适用场景**:
- 为现有空间更换材质和色彩方案
- 测试不同材质组合（地板、墙面、家具）
- 软装配饰和装饰细节设计
- 风格转换（如现代→中式、简约→奢华）
- 材质样板和情绪板生成

**典型用例**:
- 地板材质对比（木地板 vs 瓷砖 vs 地毯）
- 墙面处理方案（涂料 vs 壁纸 vs 饰面板）
- 软装配色方案（暖色 vs 冷色 vs 中性）
- 家具材质升级（实木 vs 皮革 vs 织物）
- 灯光氛围和照明方案可视化
- 装饰画、植物、抱枕等软装细节搭配

### English

**Material & Styling** is the fourth stage of the design workflow, focused on material refinement, color schemes, soft furnishings, and style customization. This stage helps designers deepen the material and style design based on existing spatial layouts.

**Use Cases**:
- Change materials and color schemes for existing spaces
- Test different material combinations (flooring, walls, furniture)
- Soft furnishings and decorative detail design
- Style transformation (e.g., modern → Chinese, minimalist → luxury)
- Material sample and mood board generation

**Typical Examples**:
- Flooring material comparison (wood vs tile vs carpet)
- Wall treatment options (paint vs wallpaper vs panels)
- Soft furnishing color schemes (warm vs cool vs neutral)
- Furniture material upgrades (solid wood vs leather vs fabric)
- Lighting atmosphere and illumination plan visualization
- Decorative details like artwork, plants, cushions coordination
