## 提示词层级 / Prompt Hierarchy

本案例提供三个层级的提示词，适应不同用户需求：

| 层级 | 适用场景 | 特点 |
|------|----------|------|
| **Level 1: 快速修正** | 赶工交图、不想深究细节 | 完全自动，AI 判断最佳调色方向 |

## Level 1: 快速修正 (Quick Fix)

**适用场景**：快速统一整体色调，让 AI 自动判断最佳调色方向。

### 中文

```
使用上传的图片作为严格的参考底图。保持所有的家具、装饰、几何结构和细节完全不变。

首先分析这张渲染图的空间类型、设计风格和预期氛围。
然后识别当前色温的具体问题（如不协调的混合光照、局部色偏）。

重新渲染图像以统一光照色温，同时：
- 保留并强化原设计的氛围意图（如温馨住宅保持暖调，现代办公保持冷调）
- 仅消除不协调的色偏和混合光照冲突
- 确保最终色调既统一和谐，又能提升空间的设计表现力

不要将画面调成平庸的中性色调，而是调成最能展现本空间设计特色的色调。
```

### English

```
Use the uploaded image as a strict reference. Keep all furniture, decorations, geometry, and details exactly unchanged.

First, analyze the space type, design style, and intended atmosphere of this render.
Then, identify the specific color temperature issues (such as clashing mixed lighting or localized color casts).

Re-render the image to unify the lighting color temperature while:
- Preserving and enhancing the original design's atmospheric intent (e.g., keep warm tones for cozy residential spaces, cool tones for modern offices)
- Only eliminating discordant color casts and mixed lighting conflicts
- Ensuring the final tone is both unified and harmonious, while elevating the space's design expression

Do not flatten the image into a bland neutral tone. Instead, grade it to the tone that best showcases this space's unique design character.
```

## Level 2: 高级元提示词 (Advanced Meta-Prompt)

**适用场景**：当您不确定如何描述当前的色偏问题时，使用此提示词让 AI 分析并生成**纯指令式**的修复方案。

### 中文

```
你是一位专业的建筑可视化后期专家。请分析上传的渲染图，识别色温和白平衡的具体缺陷。
然后，根据你的分析编写一段**修改指令提示词**。

⚠️ **关键规则（Nano Banana Pro 专用）**：
1. **纯指令模式**：只写"要做什么修改"，禁止描述画面内容。
2. **光色分离**：明确区分"光照色温"和"物体固有色"。
   - ✅ 正确：中和画面中的冷色**光线**。
   - ❌ 错误：移除背景的蓝色（这会导致蓝色家具变色）。
3. **强制保真指令**：必须包含以下保护性指令：
   - "Do not alter the intrinsic color of any furniture or materials."（不要改变任何家具或材质的固有色）
   - "Only execute color grading on the lighting layer."（仅在光照层进行调色）

**目标输出格式示例**：
"Use the original image as a strict reference. Strictly preserve all geometry and details unchanged. DO NOT change the color of existing furniture (e.g., keep the dark wood and fabric colors exact). Neutralize the cool tint in the **ambient lighting** only. Calibrate the global white balance to 4000K without affecting material textures. Enhance shadow contrast."
```

### English

```
You are a professional architectural visualization post-production expert. Analyze the uploaded render to identify specific defects in color temperature and white balance.
Then, write a **modification instruction prompt** based on your analysis.

⚠️ **Critical Rules (for Nano Banana Pro)**:
1. **Instruction-Only Mode**: Write ONLY "what to modify", NO content descriptions.
2. **Light vs. Material Separation**: Distinctly separate "lighting temperature" from "material intrinsic color".
   - ✅ Correct: Neutralize cool **lighting** cast.
   - ❌ Incorrect: Remove blue tints from background (This turns blue furniture gray).
3. **Mandatory Protection Instructions**: You MUST include:
   - "Do not alter the intrinsic color of any furniture, soft furnishings, or materials."
   - "Apply color correction ONLY to the lighting/atmosphere, NOT the objects."

**Target Output Example**:
"Use the original image as a strict reference. Strictly preserve all geometry and details unchanged. DO NOT alter the intrinsic color of any background furniture or fabrics. Neutralize the cool blue spill in the **lighting** only, not the objects. Calibrate global white balance to natural 4000K. Enhance contrast maintaining original material fidelity."
```

### 元提示词生成的实际指令示例 / Example Output from Meta-Prompt

以下是 AI 根据上述元提示词分析本案例输入图后生成的修复指令：

```
Use the original image as a strict reference. Strictly preserve all geometry and details unchanged.

DO NOT alter the intrinsic color of any furniture, soft furnishings, or materials. specifically preserving the red ottoman and wood textures. Apply color correction ONLY to the lighting/atmosphere, NOT the objects.

Neutralize the excessive cool blue/cyan spill in the shadows and floor reflections. Recalibrate the black stone materials (walls and floor) to read as neutral charcoal/black, removing the blue atmospheric tint. Harmonize the global white balance to a natural neutral tone (approx 4000K), blending the cool window light and warm interior lights without color contamination. Enhance contrast in the stone textures while keeping the lighting neutral.
```

## Level 3: 手动精调 (Manual Fine-Tuning)

**适用场景**：专业后期师，已知明确需求，需要精确控制色温值。

### 中文

```
使用上传的图片作为严格的参考底图。保持所有的家具、装饰、几何结构和细节完全不变。重新渲染图像以统一光照色温。消除原本混乱的混合光照（如过冷的窗户光或过暖的室内灯光冲突），将整体色调统一为 [指定色温值，如：3000K / 4000K / 5000K / 6500K]。确保白平衡准确，色彩自然和谐。
```

### English

```
Use the uploaded image as a strict reference. Keep all furniture, decorations, geometry, and details exactly unchanged. Re-render the image to unify the lighting color temperature. Eliminate chaotic mixed lighting (such as clashing cool window light and warm indoor lights) and unify the overall tone to [Specified Kelvin Value, e.g., 3000K / 4000K / 5000K / 6500K]. Ensure accurate white balance and natural, harmonious colors.
```

### 色温参考表 / Kelvin Reference Guide

| 色温值 | 名称 | 适用场景 |
|--------|------|----------|
| **2700K** | 暖白光 (Warm White) | 卧室、餐厅、酒店套房 |
| **3000K** | 暖白光 (Warm White) | 住宅客厅、精品酒店 |
| **4000K** | 自然白光 (Natural White) | 办公室、展厅、商业空间 |
| **5000K** | 日光白 (Daylight) | 医院、实验室、艺术馆 |
| **6500K** | 冷白光 (Cool White) | 工业空间、科技展厅 |

## Tips / 使用技巧

### 中文
- **优先使用快速修正**：对于大多数情况，AI 自动判断的结果已经足够好。
- **元提示词用于精准诊断**：当快速修正结果不满意时，使用元提示词让 AI 解释问题并生成定制方案。
- **混合光照处理**：如果原图中窗户的蓝光太强，可以在提示词中明确"中和窗户的蓝色溢出 (neutralize blue spill from windows)"。
- **保持材质**：强调"保持材质纹理 (preserve material textures)"以防止色调校正过度导致材质像"塑料"。

### English
- **Prefer Quick Fix First**: For most cases, AI's automatic judgment produces good enough results.
- **Use Meta-Prompt for Precise Diagnosis**: When Quick Fix results are unsatisfactory, use the meta-prompt to let AI explain the issue and generate a customized solution.
- **Mixed Lighting**: If the blue light from windows is too strong, explicitly state "neutralize blue spill from windows".
- **Preserve Materials**: Emphasize "preserve material textures" to prevent tone correction from making materials look plastic.
