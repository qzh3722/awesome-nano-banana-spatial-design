## 📝 Prompt / 提示词

### English

```
Enhance the lighting and atmosphere of this interior rendering while preserving all geometry and design elements exactly:

LIGHTING ENHANCEMENT:
1. Chandelier Glow: Intensify the warm glow emanating from the crystal chandeliers. Add soft light halos and natural light bloom effects around each light source.

2. Shadow Recovery: Lift the shadow areas (under tables, corners, behind screens) to reveal hidden details while maintaining depth. Avoid flat or washed-out appearance.

3. Light Falloff: Apply realistic inverse-square light falloff from each light source. Areas closer to lights should be noticeably brighter with smooth gradual dimming.

4. Ceiling Bounce: Add subtle warm light reflection on the ceiling surfaces from the chandeliers and ambient sources below.

5. Depth Enhancement: Create clear lighting separation between foreground tables (brighter) and background areas (slightly muted) to enhance spatial depth.

PRESERVATION RULES:
- Maintain EXACT geometry, furniture, decor, and color palette
- Keep the warm, luxurious atmosphere
- Do not add or remove any objects
- Preserve all material textures and finishes

OUTPUT: Photorealistic interior with enhanced lighting that feels natural and atmospheric, as if captured with professional photography lighting.
```

### 中文

```
在完全保留原有几何形状和设计元素的前提下，增强这张室内渲染图的灯光和氛围效果：

光影增强：
1. 吊灯光晕：强化水晶吊灯散发的暖色光晕。在每个光源周围添加柔和的光晕和自然的光线扩散效果。

2. 暗部恢复：提亮阴影区域（桌下、角落、屏风后方），显现隐藏的细节，同时保持画面深度，避免平淡或过度曝光。

3. 光线衰减：应用真实的平方反比光线衰减规律。靠近光源的区域应明显更亮，并呈现平滑的渐暗过渡。

4. 天花反光：在天花板表面添加来自下方吊灯和环境光的微妙暖色反射。

5. 层次增强：在前景餐桌（更亮）和背景区域（稍暗）之间创建清晰的光照层次分离，增强空间纵深感。

保真规则：
- 保持精确的几何形状、家具、装饰和色彩搭配
- 保持温馨奢华的氛围
- 不添加或移除任何物体
- 保留所有材质纹理和表面处理

输出：光影增强后的照片级室内效果，呈现自然且富有氛围感的专业摄影级灯光效果。
```

## 💡 Tips / 技巧

### Adjustment Keywords / 调整关键词

| Need | Add to Prompt | 需求 | 添加内容 |
|------|---------------|------|----------|
| Stronger glow | "dramatic light bloom" | 更强光晕 | "戏剧性光晕效果" |
| Subtle enhancement | "gentle, natural improvement" | 微调 | "轻柔自然的改善" |
| Warmer tone | "golden hour warmth" | 更暖色调 | "黄金时段的温暖感" |
| More contrast | "cinematic lighting contrast" | 更高对比 | "电影级光影对比" |

### Best Practices / 最佳实践

1. **Geometry Preservation**: The "PRESERVATION RULES" section is critical - without it, AI may modify furniture or layout
2. **Incremental Adjustment**: If result is too dramatic, add "subtle" or "gentle" to dial back intensity
3. **Style Consistency**: For consistent series, save and reuse the exact same prompt
