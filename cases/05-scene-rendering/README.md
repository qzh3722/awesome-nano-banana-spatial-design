# Scene Rendering / 场景渲染

> Final presentation renders | 最终演示效果图

## 工作流阶段说明 / Workflow Stage Description

### 中文

**场景渲染**是设计流程的第五阶段，专注于生成最终演示级别的照片级真实效果图。这个阶段的提示词帮助设计师创建高质量的透视图、人视角场景、氛围渲染，适合客户演示和营销材料。

**适用场景**:
- 生成特定视角的室内外场景效果图
- 创建带有真实光照和阴影的演示级渲染
- 制作不同时间段的场景效果（白天、黄昏、夜晚）
- 添加人物、植物等生活化元素
- 生成营销宣传用的高质量效果图

**典型用例**:
- 室内空间透视渲染（客厅、卧室、办公室）
- 建筑外观效果图（街景视角、鸟瞰视角）
- 景观场景渲染（庭院、公园、露台）
- 不同时段光照效果对比
- 季节变化效果图（春夏秋冬）
- VR全景图的关键视角渲染

### English

**Scene Rendering** is the fifth stage of the design workflow, focused on generating final presentation-quality photorealistic renders. Prompts in this stage help designers create high-quality perspective views, human-eye-level scenes, and atmospheric renders suitable for client presentations and marketing materials.

**Use Cases**:
- Generate interior/exterior scene renders from specific viewpoints
- Create presentation-quality renders with realistic lighting and shadows
- Produce scene effects at different times of day (daytime, dusk, night)
- Add lifelike elements such as people and plants
- Generate high-quality renders for marketing materials

**Typical Examples**:
- Interior space perspective renders (living room, bedroom, office)
- Architectural exterior renders (street view, bird's-eye view)
- Landscape scene renders (courtyard, park, terrace)
- Lighting effect comparisons at different times of day
- Seasonal variation renders (spring, summer, autumn, winter)
- Key viewpoint renders for VR panoramas
