## 提示词 / Prompt

### 中文版

```
将这张日间渲染图转换为写实夜景，同时严格保持所有几何形状、材质和设计元素不变：

自动夜景转换：
1. 分析场景类型（室内/室外）并智能决定合适的夜间照明
2. 窗户处理：添加温暖的室内光芒（从外向内视角）或冷调的室外夜色（从内向外视角）
3. 添加自然的人工照明效果——点亮所有可见灯具，呈现真实的光晕和光芒扩散
4. 将天空/背景转换为暮色或夜晚氛围
5. 应用符合人工照明特征的真实光线衰减和阴影
6. 在玻璃和抛光表面添加微妙的反射

严格保真：
- 保持精确的几何形状、颜色、材质和空间布局
- 不添加、移除或修改任何物体
- 保留原设计意图和氛围

输出：专业建筑摄影品质的夜景可视化效果。
```

### English Version

```
Transform this daytime rendering into a realistic night scene while strictly preserving all geometry, materials, and design elements:

AUTOMATIC NIGHT TRANSFORMATION:
1. Analyze the scene type (interior/exterior) and intelligently determine appropriate night lighting
2. For windows: Add warm interior glow (if viewing from outside) or cool exterior darkness (if viewing from inside)
3. Add natural artificial lighting effects - activate all visible light fixtures with realistic glow and bloom
4. Transform the sky/background to twilight or night atmosphere
5. Apply realistic light falloff and shadows appropriate for artificial lighting
6. Add subtle reflections on glass and polished surfaces

STRICT PRESERVATION:
- Maintain exact geometry, colors, materials, and spatial layout
- Do not add, remove, or modify any objects
- Preserve the original design intent and atmosphere

OUTPUT: Professional architectural photography-quality night visualization.
```

## Tips / 使用技巧

### 中文

- **技巧1**: **零配置通用** - 此提示词适用于室内和室外场景，AI 会自动判断场景类型并应用合适的夜间照明。
- **技巧2**: **窗户智能处理** - AI 会根据视角自动决定窗户是发光（外视内）还是显示夜色（内视外）。
- **技巧3**: **灯具自动激活** - 无需描述具体灯具类型，AI 会识别画面中的所有灯具并自然点亮。

### English

- **Tip 1**: **Zero-Config Universal** - This prompt works for both interior and exterior scenes. AI automatically determines scene type and applies appropriate night lighting.
- **Tip 2**: **Smart Window Handling** - AI automatically decides whether windows emit light (exterior view) or show night sky (interior view) based on perspective.
- **Tip 3**: **Auto Light Activation** - No need to describe specific fixtures. AI identifies all visible light sources and activates them naturally.
