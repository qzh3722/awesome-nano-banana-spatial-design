## Tips / 使用技巧

### 中文

- **技巧1**: **明确光色温** - 使用具体的色温值（如 `3000K` for warm white, `4000K` for neutral white）来控制室内灯光氛围。
- **技巧2**: **蓝色时刻 (Blue Hour)** - 关键词 `blue hour` 或 `twilight` 能产生最迷人的深蓝夜空背景，与暖色室内光形成冷暖对比。
- **技巧3**: **结构保持** - 务必强调 `Maintain the exact architectural structure`，防止AI在转换光照时修改建筑造型。

### English

- **Tip 1**: **Specify Color Temperature** - Use specific Kelvin values (e.g., `3000K` for warm white, `4000K` for neutral white) to control interior lighting atmosphere.
- **Tip 2**: **Blue Hour** - The keyword `blue hour` or `twilight` creates the most appealing deep blue sky background, creating a warm-cool contrast with interior lights.
- **Tip 3**: **Structure Preservation** - Always emphasize `Maintain the exact architectural structure` to prevent the AI from altering the building form while changing the lighting.
